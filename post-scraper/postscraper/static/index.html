Topics

Search LinkedIn for posts about a topic, from anyone, and rank them by engagement. The people behind the best posts show up under Creators.

LinkedIn decides which posts its search returns, so results lean towards what it thinks is relevant. Searching the same topic again later adds new posts and updates the numbers on old ones.

Creators

People whose posts came up in your topic searches, best first. Add the good ones to your accounts so every run collects their newest posts.
