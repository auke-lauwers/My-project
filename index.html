FAQ

What counts as a qualified call?

A real decision-maker at a company that matches the profile we agree on, and who shows up. If a booked call doesn't fit, you flag it and it doesn't count. We only bill for calls that are held.

How does pricing work?

Performance-based. You pay per qualified call, not a retainer, plus a small setup fee that covers your domains and inboxes. Exact numbers depend on your market and what a client is worth to you. We set them on the call.

Will this put my domain at risk?

No. Your main domain never sends. We run separate warmed domains with conservative limits and daily monitoring.

How soon do calls start?

Two to three weeks to build and warm everything. Then sending begins. Interested replies come in the first weeks, calls shortly after.

What do I have to do?

A short onboarding call, then approve the copy and lists. After that, you take the calls.

What happens if I leave?

You keep the assets. Domains, lists, and copy transfer to you.
